# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Technical hiring teams, especially senior engineers and engineering hiring managers, evaluating Navin Kumar Yadav for software-engineering roles.

## Product Purpose

A personal software-engineering portfolio that demonstrates product engineering, system design, and developer-tool craftsmanship. A successful visit gives a technical evaluator enough credible evidence to initiate an interview or contact conversation.

## Positioning

Navin connects polished product interfaces to the APIs, services, data, automation, and engineering decisions underneath them.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan a public product artifact, assess system-thinking evidence, inspect selected project stories, and decide whether to contact or interview Navin.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The homepage is the current product surface.
- SeeMyUI is the primary public case study.
- Employer-owned projects remain anonymous and contain no confidential claims.
- Contact, resume, social, and project links remain honest placeholders until verified.
- The existing Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Motion, and GSAP stack remains.

## Brand Commitments

Use concise, confident, technical, natural language. The experience should feel mature and distinctive without becoming an animation reel. Preserve the idea of topology connecting interface to systems, but not the former atlas styling.

## Evidence on Hand

- A real SeeMyUI product screenshot at `public/projects/seemyui/studio.png`.
- Verified project descriptions in `CONTENT.md` and `src/data`.
- No verified metrics, testimonials, employer details, or contact links; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Lead with artifacts and engineering decisions.
- Reveal the systems beneath visible product behavior.
- Make confidential work credible without oversharing.
- Optimize for rapid technical evaluation and contact conversion.
- Prefer one memorable, purposeful interaction over ambient spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, usable touch targets, reduced-motion support, and complete information without animation are required.
