## Design-specificity verdict

Authored and coherent, with a distinctive systems-atlas language, but not yet inimitable. The project transformation vocabulary is specific; the experience often labels Propagation, Isolation, Sequence, Fan-out, and Convergence instead of making the visitor experience those behaviors.

The deterministic scan returned no source findings. Runtime overlays flagged undersized technical text, low-contrast blueprint metadata, extreme display tracking, and heuristic text-overflow warnings. The warm cream palette and selective mono typography are intentional; the microtype findings were credible.

## Priority issues

- **P0 — Contact dead end:** The closing invitation has no verified contact route. Content must be supplied; it should not be invented.
- **P1 — Grammars depicted, not enacted:** Only Propagation had meaningful motion, making later bespoke diagrams feel static.
- **P1 — Evidence becomes less verifiable:** Anonymous projects need public-safe proof, constraints, or responsibility boundaries rather than invented metrics.
- **P1 — Microtype below the premium floor:** Technical labels were often 9–10px and carried important meaning.
- **P2 — Repeated chapter skeleton:** Tenant, Workflow, Document, and Capabilities repeated the same header/diagram/closing composition.
- **P2 — Topology discoverability:** The hero system can look decorative and its detail can sit too low in the viewport.

## Persona red flags

- **Jordan, first-time visitor:** Topology interactivity and transformation vocabulary are not self-evident.
- **Riley, skeptical verifier:** Anonymous work lacks dates, role boundaries, and public-safe proof artifacts.
- **Sam, accessibility-dependent visitor:** Small blueprint labels and faint meaningful lines increase reading effort.

## Questions considered

- Can each project grammar control its entire chapter rather than only its diagram?
- Can motion explain structure without simulating live product execution?
- Can Convergence read as a resolving chapter instead of an inventory table?
