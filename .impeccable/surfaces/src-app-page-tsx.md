# Homepage — Product Specimen

## Surface

- Mode: Experience with a persuasive hiring outcome.
- Audience: senior engineers and technical hiring managers.
- Job: understand the product artifact, inspect the engineering beneath it, and decide to initiate contact.
- Primary proof: the real SeeMyUI studio screenshot plus verified architectural/project descriptions.
- Primary action: contact/resume slots remain visibly unavailable until verified.

## Approved Direction

- World: Product Specimen — an asymmetric optical inspection table for product and system evidence.
- Approved homepage composition: `.impeccable/mocks/product-specimen/approved.png`.
- User-selected hero composition: `.impeccable/mocks/product-specimen/hero-approved.png`; the hero contains no SeeMyUI screenshot.
- Supporting references: `hero.png`, `seemyui.png`, `systems.png`, `capabilities.png`, and `about-contact.png` in the same directory.
- Memorable moment: an inspection probe travels from a visible SeeMyUI decision into Interface, API, Services, Data, and Automation.
- Do not literalize any generated location, availability, contact detail, employer, metric, or technology absent from repository truth.

## Visual Record

- Ground: `#F3F1EB`; raised artifact field: `#F8F6F4`; graphite: `#171716`; muted graphite: `#66645F`; rules: `#C9C5BC`; signal cobalt: `#3157F5`.
- Component grammar: open 12-column layouts, 1px rules, square specimen frames, no nested cards, no decorative shadows.
- Corner language: square by default; 4px only for small controls; the circular inspection lens is the sole round signature form.
- Type: Geist sans, display 60–96px/0.94 with tracking no tighter than -0.04em; body 16–20px/1.5; mono 11–13px only for measurements and layers.
- Spacing: roughly 50px desktop gutters, 20px mobile gutters, 128–192px chapter spacing, close internal groups.

## Responsive and Accessibility

- Desktop uses the asymmetric specimen composition; tablet reduces the lens and moves topology below the artifact.
- Mobile removes pinning, presents the artifact full-width, and renders the topology as ordered readable steps.
- All controls are keyboard operable with visible focus, 44px touch targets, semantic headings, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
